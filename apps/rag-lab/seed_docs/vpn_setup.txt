Remote VPN Configuration Guide — NimbleTech IT
================================================
To connect to the corporate network remotely:
1. Download GlobalProtect client from https://vpn.nimbletech.local
2. Enter portal address: vpn.nimbletech.local
3. Sign in with your AD credentials (firstname.lastname@nimbletech.com)
4. Complete MFA via Duo push notification
5. You will be assigned an IP in the 10.50.0.0/16 range
Troubleshooting:
- Connection refused: ensure port 443 outbound is open
- MFA timeout: re-register your Duo token at https://duo.nimbletech.local/enroll
- Credential rejected: contact IT helpdesk at ext. 4242
Split Tunneling:
Only traffic to 10.0.0.0/8 and *.nimbletech.local is tunneled.
Internet traffic exits via your local ISP.

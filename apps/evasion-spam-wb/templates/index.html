🔓 ML Evasion — GoodWords Attack
Naive Bayes GoodWords Evasion White-Box Black-Box
Section 1: Objective ▼

The /api/classify endpoint uses a Multinomial Naive Bayes model for spam detection. The goal is to exploit the additive scoring nature of NB by injecting high-goodness tokens ("goodwords") that pull spam messages toward the ham boundary.

Why it matters: Real spam filters in production use similar ML models. Understanding GoodWords attacks reveals why naive ML classifiers need layered defenses — a single model score is never enough.
Section 2: Attack Path (Production — Vulnerable) ▼

Step 1 - Confirm baseline:

curl -s http://localhost:5040/api/classify -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"text":"WINNER!! As a valued network customer you have been selected to receive a £900 prize reward! To claim call 09061701461.","mode":"vulnerable"}' | python3 -m json.tool

Expected: SPAM, spam_prob ≈ 0.99

Step 2 - Extract GoodWords (white-box):

curl -s "http://localhost:5040/api/goodwords?k=30" | python3 -m json.tool

Step 3 - Run boundary sweep:

curl -s http://localhost:5040/api/sweep -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"counts":[0,5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40],"mode":"vulnerable"}' | python3 -m json.tool

Step 4 - Craft evasion payload:

WORDS=$(curl -s "http://localhost:5040/api/goodwords?k=20" | python3 -c "import sys,json; print(' '.join(w['word'] for w in json.load(sys.stdin)['words']))")
curl -s http://localhost:5040/api/classify -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"text\":\"WINNER!! You won £900! Call 09061701461. $WORDS\",\"mode\":\"vulnerable\"}" | python3 -m json.tool

Step 5 - Black-box oracle attack:

curl -s http://localhost:5040/api/blackbox -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"budget":40,"mode":"vulnerable"}' | python3 -m json.tool
Section 3: Escalation — Staging (Hardened) ▼

Staging mode adds token_repetition and long_message_with_spam_core defenses.

Bypass idea: use diverse goodwords (no repetition), keep message short.

Section 4: Escalation — Compliance (Guardrailed) ▼

Compliance mode adds spam_keyword_bumper and spam_head_benign_tail defenses.

Bypass idea: obfuscate spam keywords themselves — goodwords alone can't beat compliance mode.

Section 5: Defense Takeaways ▼
  • Never rely on raw score alone
  • Layer heuristics + keyword policy
  • Length & repetition normalization
  • Ensemble / adversarial training is the durable fix
  • Rate-limit the oracle (query budgets)
